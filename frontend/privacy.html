Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password (hashed)
  • Academic data: maths, physics, chemistry marks, community category
  • Usage data: prediction history, payment status
  • Device data: browser type, IP address (for rate limiting only)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate college seat predictions personalised to you
  • To manage your account and payment status
  • To improve prediction accuracy using anonymised data
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties

3. Payments

  • Payments are processed by Razorpay
  • We do not store your card or bank details
  • Payment records are kept for billing and support purposes

4. Data Retention

  • Your account and prediction data is retained as long as your account is active
  • You can request deletion of your account by contacting us

5. Cookies

  • We use localStorage to store your login session token
  • No third-party tracking cookies are used

6. Contact Us

For privacy concerns, email: support@pickmyseat.in
