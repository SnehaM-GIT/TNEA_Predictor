1. Introduction

  • This Privacy Policy explains how PickMySeat ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data of users ("you") of pickmyseat.in (the "Service"), in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), to the extent applicable.
  • By using the Service, you consent to the data practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: full name, email address, mobile number, and password (stored in hashed form only — we never store passwords in plain text).
  • Academic data: Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry marks, cut-off/aggregate scores, and optionally your TNEA rank.
  • Community/reservation category: this is treated as sensitive personal data under this Policy because of its association with caste-based reservation status. It is collected solely to generate accurate, category-specific admission predictions and is processed with additional safeguards described in Section 4.
  • Usage data: prediction history, feature usage, and payment/subscription status (Grade 1/2/3).
  • Technical data: browser type, device type, and IP address, collected solely for rate limiting, fraud prevention, and security purposes.
  • Payment metadata: transaction ID, payment status, and amount (via Razorpay). We do not collect or store card numbers, UPI IDs, or bank account details.

3. Legal Basis and Consent

  • We process your personal data on the basis of your explicit, informed consent, given at the time of registration via an affirmative checkbox action, and for the specific purposes stated in this Policy.
  • You may withdraw consent at any time by requesting account deletion (Section 8), though this will mean you can no longer use the Service.
  • We collect only the data necessary for the purposes described (data minimisation) and do not use your academic or community data for any purpose beyond generating your predictions and improving model accuracy (in anonymised, aggregated form).

4. Sensitive Personal Data Safeguards

  • Community/category data is stored encrypted at rest and in transit, and access is restricted to the minimum systems and personnel required to generate predictions.
  • We do not use community/category data for advertising, profiling for marketing purposes, or any purpose unrelated to seat prediction.
  • We do not disclose your community/category data to any third party except processors strictly necessary to operate the Service (Section 7), and never for commercial resale.

5. Minors and Parental Consent

  • We recognise that many users of the Service are under 18 years of age. Where a user is a minor, use of the Service and provision of personal data must be authorised and consented to by a parent or lawful guardian.
  • We do not knowingly carry out tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children, and we do not process minors' data in any manner likely to cause harm to them.
  • A parent or guardian may contact us at support.pickmyseat@gmail.com to review, correct, or request deletion of a minor's data at any time.

6. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalised college and branch seat predictions for TNEA counselling.
  • To create and manage your account, authenticate logins, and track payment/subscription status.
  • To send essential transactional communications (e.g., account verification, password resets, payment confirmations) via email.
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • To improve prediction model accuracy using anonymised and aggregated data that cannot reasonably be used to re-identify you.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for their marketing purposes.

  • Railway (hosting/infrastructure provider) — for hosting our backend application and database. Data may be stored on servers located outside India; by using the Service you consent to this transfer, which is protected by industry-standard security controls.
  • We may disclose personal data if required to do so by law, court order, or a valid request from a government or regulatory authority.
  • In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your account and prediction data is retained for as long as your account remains active, and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations (typically up to 8 years for payment records, as required under Indian law).
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by emailing support.pickmyseat@gmail.com. We will act on verified deletion requests within 30 days, except for data we are legally required to retain.
  • Anonymised, aggregated data used for model training is not personally identifiable and is not subject to deletion requests.
